# Готель «Монте Крісто»

Live site: https://montecristo.hotelup.work

## About
«Монте Крісто» is a hotel and apartments property in Kamianets-Podilskyi, Ukraine, located at vul. Lesi Ukrainky, 23 (the hotel entrance is from vul. Danyla Halytskoho). It combines short-stay hotel rooms with a sauna and hamam for relaxation, plus long-term apartment rental for guests who need a longer stay in the city. The positioning is flexible: stay for one night or for a month, depending on the format you choose.

## Amenities
- Hotel rooms for short stays (1-3 nights and longer)
- Sauna
- Hamam
- Long-term rental apartments
